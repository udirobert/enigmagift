## 2. Why personalised gifts work (psychology)

- **Name-letter effect** — personalisation triggers the brain's reward centre.
- Personalisation **signals effort and care**; recipient feels *seen*.
- Personalised gifts are **kept long-term** and rated higher in emotional worth
  than generic ones — they become symbols of a shared memory.

Implication: the product must *demonstrate effort*. A thing that obviously took
thought beats a thing that is merely pretty. **Show the work.**

## 3. Freemium mechanics

- Free tier must deliver the **core value**, not a teaser — emotional commitment
  before the ask is what converts.
- Hard paywalls convert better but reach less; free trials reach more, convert worse.
- Best shape: let them feel the thing, then charge for **keeping/sending** it.

Implication: give away the *moment*. Charge for the *artefact* and the *delivery*.

## 4. Distribution signal

- Gift **reveal/reaction** videos are a proven, repeating TikTok format.
- Oriane: top AI-tool creators all use **comment-gating** ("comment ADS and I'll
  send the link") — reach without ad spend.
- Calendar: Spotify Wrapped lands late Nov/early Dec and primes the entire
  internet to share personalised stat cards. Gifting peaks straight after,
  running right through **16 Dec = Ship or Die's end date**.

## 5. Local portfolio DNA worth carrying (concepts only — fresh code)

> Ship or Die bans reused code/designs. These are *patterns we already proved*,
> to be rebuilt clean.

- **jejak** — "**guess first, then reveal**": the user commits a guess, *then*
  the truth is shown against it. Also: the trace **arrives unfinished**, showing
  each step with real figures attached, and auto-writes a shareable postcard.
  → The single best mechanic in the portfolio. This is where "enigma" comes from.
- **futureselves** — voice-driven **ritual**; "transmission" framing; the
  *Last Voicemail* (a cinematic voicemail from a future you). Explicitly built on
  the Nikita Bier ladder: core loop → shares → invites → pays.
- **wowowify** — composite an *exact* asset into generated artwork without the
  model redrawing it. Critical for keepsakes: a real face/photo must stay real.
- **posterly** — HTML → print-ready PDF at exact dimensions. Printable keepsake.
- **nuncio / auteur** — personalised video + voice production pipeline.

## 6. Open risks

- "AI slop gift" perception — must feel crafted, not generated.
- Shipping physical items is banned by Ship or Die and can't deliver before the
  MMM freeze — **digital-only**, with print-it-yourself as the physical bridge.
- Buyers must be strangers (both comps). No friends/family purchases count.
