privacy

last updated: 21 november 2026 · plain-language, no dark patterns

enigmagift turns your photographs into a treasure hunt only one person can solve. photographs are personal — often of the recipient and other people — so this page tells you exactly what we collect, who else sees it, how long we keep it, and how to delete it. if you upload photos, you confirm you have the right to share them and that the people in them know.

what we collect

  • the photos you upload (5–12 per gift) and the choices you make while building the gift (clues, hiding spot, tier).
  • the recipient link you share (a random token in the URL, e.g. /t/…) and the unlock code (stored only as a one-way scrypt hash).
  • first-party usage events (page views, round picks, “sealed”, “paid”) on our own server, to understand where gifts get stuck. we run no third-party analytics, ads, or trackers.
  • payment is handled entirely by Stripe (payment links). card numbers never reach our servers.
  • we use no cookies. your in-progress gift id lives in your browser’s localStorage/sessionStorage only, on your device.

why, and who else processes it

your photos are sent to the AI providers below solely to generate your gift (reading the photos into clues, building the 3D world and the keepsake portrait). we do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

what we do to protect it

  • EXIF metadata (including GPS location) is stripped from uploaded JPEG/PNG files on our server before anything reads them; the app also re-encodes photos in your browser before upload.
  • the recipient only ever holds a random link token: photos, the 3D world and the portrait are served through token-scoped URLs; the answer key to the rounds never leaves the server.
  • unlock codes are stored as scrypt hashes; the Stripe webhook signature is verified before any payment is recorded; payment confirmation from your browser alone is not trusted.
  • TLS everywhere, a strict Content-Security-Policy, Referrer-Policy: no-referrer (so the link token can’t leak to other sites), rate limiting on uploads, answers and code attempts, and CORS restricted to our own domains.
  • secrets (API keys, webhook secret) live only in encrypted server secrets, never in the app or the repo.

how long we keep it

  • unsealed drafts (gifts never sent) are deleted automatically after 7 days, photos included.
  • sealed gifts (photos, world, portrait, link) are kept until the buyer deletes the gift or revokes the link — there is currently no automatic expiry for sealed gifts; we’ll email a reminder and add auto-expiry if that changes.
  • payment records are kept as long as tax law requires (Stripe holds the card data; we see only amounts and statuses).

your rights

  • delete: the buyer can delete a gift at any time (removes photos, world, link) and revoke the recipient link so it stops opening. deleting the gift is the fastest erasure we can offer.
  • access / export: everything we hold about a gift is visible to the buyer in the editor; email us for a full JSON export.
  • rectification / complaint: email us and, if you’re in the UK/EU, you also have the right to complain to the ICO.

children

you must be 16+ (UK/EU) to buy. if photos include children, the buyer confirms they have parental responsibility / the right to share those images.
