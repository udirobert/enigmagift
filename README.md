# EnigmaGift

> A gift that only one person in the world can open. The lock is made of your own photographs.

Give it photos of the two of you. It looks closely, finds the details — and the one
pattern running through all of them that neither of you ever noticed. That becomes a
chain of clues only they can solve, and solving it *is* the gift.
